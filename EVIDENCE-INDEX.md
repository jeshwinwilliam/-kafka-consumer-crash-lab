## Important boundaries

The archive is evidence for the article's scoped observations; it is not a
claim of universal exactly-once processing or production readiness. The
archive preserves historical absolute paths and is therefore **review-only**
until a privacy-reviewed public derivative and its new identity are approved.
The original archive SHA-256 above must remain associated with the unchanged
historical bytes.
